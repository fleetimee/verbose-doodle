# Fleetime Labs mascot

Fleetime Labs' curious technical companion, helping developers explore, inspect, and understand their tools.

## Design direction

The mascot has an **Uma Musume-like horse-girl aesthetic**: an expressive anime woman with upright horse ears, a long flowing tail, and a lively ponytail. This describes the intended visual influence, rather than identifying her as a specific Uma Musume character. Her identity within Fleetime Labs comes from the technical operator outfit, receipt-shaped hair clip, mint-teal accents, and code tablet.

Her personality is approachable, capable, curious, and playful. Poses should communicate a recognizable task, such as greeting the user, inspecting something, or thinking through a problem.

## Standard illustration

An anime-style horse-girl developer assistant with adult proportions, warm brown hair in a side ponytail, mint-teal streaks, expressive amber-green eyes, and upright brown ears with cream inner tufts. Her long brown tail carries matching teal streaks and a teal tip.

She wears a cream cropped utility jacket over a charcoal high-neck outfit, with mint-teal zippers, buckles, straps, and small code-brace details. Fingerless gloves, a utility belt, shorts, dark legwear, and sturdy sneakers complete the practical operator outfit.

The receipt-shaped hair clip connects her to billing. A charcoal tablet marked with mint `{}` braces connects her to developer tools. The search pose adds a teal-rimmed magnifying glass while retaining the tablet and the same character design.

Use polished anime linework, soft cel shading, and clear facial expressions. Preserve her brown, mint-teal, cream, charcoal, and natural skin colors across poses.

**Short description:** A curious horse-girl technical assistant with brown-and-teal hair, a cream utility jacket, and a code tablet.

## Chibi illustration

The chibi mascot is the same character rendered with an oversized head, a compact body, short limbs, rounded cheeks, and larger amber-green eyes. Her upright horse ears, brown side ponytail, mint-teal hair streaks, receipt hair clip, and flowing tail keep her recognizable at small sizes.

Simplify the cream jacket, charcoal outfit, teal hardware, fingerless gloves, and sneakers without changing their colors or overall design. The code tablet becomes a prominent prop held in both hands. Keep her expression friendly and attentive, with a small smile or a focused thinking look.

Chibi proportions are a stylization of the same mascot, not a separate character. Use this version for compact companion illustrations, greetings, and thinking states where the standard illustration would lose detail.

**Short description:** A pocket-sized chibi version of the horse-girl assistant, with big expressive eyes, brown-and-teal hair, and her signature code tablet.

Reference: [Chibi mascot](public/brand/biller-operator-mascot-chibi.png).

## Consistency and UI use

- Preserve the horse-girl identity, hair and tail colors, receipt clip, operator outfit, and code-tablet motif.
- Keep the standard and chibi versions recognizable as the same character.
- Use props and poses to explain the current task. Avoid unrelated costume changes.
- Keep decorative illustrations behind content and out of pointer and keyboard interactions.
- Use genuine alpha transparency for background artwork. A painted checkerboard is not transparency.
- Preserve original colors in both light and dark themes. Adjust opacity when needed; do not invert colors, apply grayscale, or use screen blending to hide a background.
- Keep search results, selection states, and descriptions readable over decorative artwork.
